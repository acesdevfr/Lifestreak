Privacy policy

What Lifestreak is. A tool to time your activities, count your habits and keep streaks. Everything you record is stored only on your device.

No servers, no personal data. We have no servers or databases. Lifestreak doesn’t collect, send or sell your information: your records, username, password and photo never leave your phone. The app’s only internet connection is to load its font.

Optional account. Creating an account is optional and works only on this device. Your password can’t be recovered. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, accounts and records are deleted.

Your wellbeing comes first. Lifestreak isn’t designed to push anyone to do harmful things, whatever category you choose. Counters only track habits; if you mark something as bad, the app just reminds you to cut back. It isn’t medical advice: if you want to quit something that affects your health, talk to a professional.

Notifications and control. Reminders are created on your device, with no server, and you can turn them off in your phone’s settings. You can edit your profile or delete categories, counters and your account at any time, and everything is erased from the device.
