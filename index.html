Everything you need to build better habits

Timed activities

Start a category and the clock keeps running until you stop it, even if you leave the app.

Daily streaks

Use a category at least once a day to grow your streak. Your first day counts from tomorrow.

Counters

Count how many times you do anything, once or several times a day. Mark it as good to keep up or bad to cut back.

Calendar

See what you did and what is coming in a clean, colorful calendar. Add activities to any future day.

Flexible reminders

Set the exact time with AM/PM, then pick every day, certain weekdays or specific dates.

Optional local accounts

Create a username, password and profile photo to keep records separate on a shared phone. Everything stays on your device.

5 languages

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German. Switch any time inside the app.

Private by design

No servers and no tracking. Your records never leave your phone.

How it works

1

Pick or create a category

Sleep and Walk come built in. Add your own timed activities or counters.

2

Start, count, remind

Tap Start to run the timer, tap +1 to count, and set reminders so you never forget.

3

Keep your streak

Come back every day. Your calendar and records show how far you have come.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Accounts are optional and are stored only on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Lifestreak has no servers or databases.

How do I install it?

Download the APK and open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager, then install. The iOS version is coming soon.

Is it medical advice?

No. Lifestreak is a habit tool, not medical or psychological advice. If something affects your health, talk to a professional.
